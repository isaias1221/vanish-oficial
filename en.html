Secure Installation Guide

Steps to bypass the Google Play Protect block.

IMPORTANT

Vanish is a decentralized application focused on extreme privacy. It is not available on the Google Play Store to ensure no corporation tracks its downloads, censors it, or links its usage to your personal Google account.

Due to this technical independence, your phone will show a Google Play Protect alert warning that it's an "unknown" app. This is a standard Android mechanism for any external APK file.

1

Open Google Play Store

Open the official store and tap your profile picture in the top right corner.

2

Go to "Play Protect"

Select Play Protect in the menu and then tap the gear icon (⚙️) at the top right.

3

Disable scanning

Turn off the first option that says "Scan apps with Play Protect".

4

Install the Vanish APK

Open the downloaded Vanish.1.0.final.apk file and install it. Done! You can turn Play Protect back on if you wish.

🧬 Volatile Identity & Panic Button

Upon entry, you are assigned a random alias (e.g., Legionary 4092). Your identity and active chats are kept alive by your device's local cache memory. If you clear the app data from Android settings, your identity is irrevocably destroyed.

Panic Button: Long-press the top "Vanish" title for 1 second to instantly activate Safe Mode, hide sensitive images, and return to the splash screen.

🏠 Global Wall & Filters

The top radar allows you to isolate the noise. Filter by Posts, Polls, or Sales (with automatic currency sub-filters: ARS, USD, BTC).

If the wall is too crowded, go to Settings > Interface and change the density to "Compact" or enable "Data Saver" to hide images until tapped. A red dot 🔴 will indicate active replies or sales.

⏳ Lifespan & Self-Destruction

Everything you create has a configurable expiration date (from 1 to 72 hours). If you attach heavy photos, the network restricts the lifespan to 24 hours for security.

When the timer reaches 00:00:00, the server executes an atomic purge. The post, attached images, votes, and all associated comments are physically deleted from the database leaving no backups.

💬 Dark Tunnels (Business)

When publishing a Sale, interested parties do not reply in public. Tapping "Negotiate" opens an encrypted Private Tunnel between both parties. As a seller, you have a tactical console to mark the item as "Sold Out" or pin (📌) a specific buyer so you don't lose their chat.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Vanish read my messages?

No. The network is decentralized. We do not store your information for analysis or create profiles.

What happens if I clear the app data?

The app uses local cache memory to save your active chats and identity. If you clear the cache from Android settings, you will lose access to your sales and private tunnels forever.

Why don't you ask for an email or password?

We believe in true anonymity. Asking for an email creates a forensic tracking point. In Vanish, you are a temporary ghost on the network.
